Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our booking process.

Why is a KES 300 deposit required?

The deposit holds your exact chair slot and protects our barbers against last-minute no-shows. The KES 300 is automatically deducted from your final bill when you arrive at the shop.

Can I reschedule if my plans change?

Yes! You can reschedule up to 2 hours before your appointment time via WhatsApp or by calling our desk at 0724 590 868 without losing your deposit.

How do House Calls work?

Kitiki Sam brings a fully equipped mobile kit (cordless clippers, shavers, sanitizers, drop cloths, and premium styling oils) directly to your home, office, or hotel in Nairobi. Select 'VIP House Call' in our services menu.
